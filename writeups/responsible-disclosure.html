Selected work / 2024

Responsible disclosure of an access-control issue

A short public account of a privately reported finding, centered on protecting the people who rely on the service.

The finding

While studying a mobile service, I identified an authorization weakness that could expose another person's private records. Seeing the potential impact on real users changed how I thought about security research: confirming a problem was only the first step; handling it responsibly mattered more.

What I did

I documented the finding and submitted it privately through the China National Vulnerability Database (CNVD). The submission was assigned a CNVD record. This confirms documentation of the report; it is not a claim that the issue was fixed or that the service is currently vulnerable.

Disclosure boundary

This public version omits the affected service's identity, live addresses, request details, screenshots, identifiers, and any personal data. I do not publish a reproduction guide. Supporting documentation can be discussed privately with an appropriate reviewer, subject to the affected party's disclosure requirements.

What stayed with me

Responsible security work means collecting only what is necessary, reporting through the right channel, and keeping users' privacy ahead of the excitement of a discovery. I bring that approach to research and to the students I mentor.
