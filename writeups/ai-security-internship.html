Test Data & Log Collection

My contribution to an AI-assisted cybersecurity platform: collecting the data used to support analysis and testing.

My responsibility

During my July 2026 internship at a client‑facing cybersecurity firm based in Hangzhou, I worked under the founder and CEO and collected test datasets and production logs for an AI-assisted cybersecurity analysis platform. My contribution was data collection to support the team’s engineering work.

Why the data mattered

Attack-path analysis depends on the evidence available to it. Test datasets give a team controlled cases to examine, while production logs provide operational context. Collecting those materials supports the team’s work on alert analysis and attack-path reconstruction.

Understanding incomplete evidence

The project’s offline examples distinguish complete evidence from cases with missing execution records, missing connection records, or alerts alone. Those cases illustrate a practical limit: an alert may justify further investigation without proving what happened on a host. This describes the test context, not a claim that I designed the analysis engine or measured its accuracy.

Public scope

This overview describes my role and the purpose of the data collection. Production logs, customer information, company source code, internal architecture, and internal test results are not included.
